26524 FEATURE SCOPE
- Continuous rear-camera pinch zoom across optical lens anchors in MOTION mode.
- Other modes retain their existing optical lens buttons; continuous digital zoom
  is intentionally not exposed there until their separate RAW/JPEG output geometry
  can be made viewfinder-identical without touching Motion IQ.
- Live zoom number is rendered inside the optical lens button that owns
  the current zoom interval.
- Rear system with >=1 true tele anchor (>=1.5x): cap 50.0x.
- Rear system with no tele anchor: cap 20.0x.
- Device minimum follows the smallest detected rear optical anchor.
- Camera2 handles supported local zoom first.
- Actual CaptureResult zoom/crop is reconciled continuously; residual GPU zoom follows what the HAL really delivered.
- A GL preview crop supplies only residual zoom beyond the HAL limit.
- Motion JPEG performs the full local zoom after the RAW merge and keeps
  the existing native/binned output dimensions.
- Ultra HDR full-resolution gain map uses identical zoom geometry.
- Motion DNG pixel dimensions/data remain the full stacked native/binned
  domain; 26523 DNG writer/support math are byte-identical.
- 26523 AF/AE touch mapping and real AF lock are preserved; only the
  post-HAL residual preview zoom is folded into touch coordinates.

SPEED PROTECTION
