What 26526 actively changes
---------------------------
Exactly two runtime files may change:
1. app/src/main/java/com/particlesdevs/photoncamera/control/IrisZoomController.java
2. app/src/main/java/com/particlesdevs/photoncamera/capture/CaptureController.java

Zoom correction:
- Camera2 is the sole live preview geometry authority while requested local zoom is
  inside the advertised Camera2 hardware/digital range.
- Software residual is derived only from requested local zoom divided by the static
  advertised Camera2 maximum, never from asynchronous CaptureResult catch-up.
- CaptureResult remains actual-HAL telemetry but cannot change live software preview crop.
- Result identity is bound to CameraCaptureSession.getDevice().getId(), not mutable
  PreferenceKeys or a controller camera field.
- Physical handoff has separate active and pending owner/anchor state.
- Pending owner becomes active only after a valid result arrives from that session.
- Old-session results cannot commit the new owner.
- Generic 2% directional hysteresis around dynamically discovered optical anchors prevents
  boundary restart chatter.
- Fast gestures may directly request the highest appropriate optical anchor instead of
  forcing serial intermediate restarts.
- No Xiaomi camera IDs or fixed lens ratios are hardcoded.
