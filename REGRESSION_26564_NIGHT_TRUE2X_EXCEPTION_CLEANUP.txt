IRIS 26564 PERMANENT REGRESSION — NIGHT TRUE2X EXCEPTION CLEANUP

Failure found during candidate audit:
After PhotonMotionMgc1271Bridge handed true2xLinearRgbPath/true2xRenderRgbPath to
IrisNightProcessor, an exception before the normal post-publication deletes could leave
those disk-backed carriers orphaned. The outer finally block had no references to them.

Permanent condition for every applicable future handoff:
1. Immediately after reconstruct() returns, Night must retain cleanup references to both
   true-2x carrier paths when present.
2. The outer finally must deleteIfExists both paths, independent of success/failure.
3. Success-path deletion may remain but cannot be the sole cleanup owner.
4. The DNG write must occur before pristine-linear deletion and final JPEG/JPEG-R must
   consume the render derivative before deletion.
5. Regression fails if carrier cleanup exists only on the success path.
