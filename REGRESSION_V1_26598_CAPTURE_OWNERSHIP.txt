PHOTON 26598 V1.1 MOTION CAPTURE OWNERSHIP PERMANENT REGRESSIONS

REAL DEVICE FAILURES
1. 26597 accepted a shutter press while a prior immutable Motion batch was processing, logged that the shutter "waits", then returned and permanently discarded the press.
2. IRIS_26593_TOTAL_FRAME_TIMEOUT occurred with shutter-frozen NORMAL top-up plans, including a real case with normalAtPress=4 / missingNormals=11 and another with normalAtPress=14 / missingNormals=1.
3. Successful captures proved the deeper ownership defect: NORMALs counted at shutter could be evicted from the rolling ZSL ring and silently replaced by unrelated post-shutter repeating RAWs.

PERMANENT CONTRACT
- Slider N remains the exact total physical RAW budget, 1..30. SHORT/LONG consume reserved slots; no hidden extra frames.
- Example N=15 with SHORT+LONG is exactly 13 NORMAL + 1 SHORT + 1 LONG.
- If 8 eligible NORMALs exist at shutter, those exact 8 become generation-owned and only 5 NORMAL + SHORT + LONG remain post-shutter.
- Every accepted pre-shutter NORMAL is removed from rolling-ring ownership and cannot be evicted/substituted.
- Every missing NORMAL is an explicit generation-owned timestamp ticket; unrelated repeating RAWs can never satisfy the capture plan.
- Motion ImageReader drains all currently queued RAWs per callback so coalesced notifications cannot strand tail frames.
- Completion is exact-slot based; NORMAL exposure-group compatibility remains shutter-frozen. No duplicated frame, mismatched fallback, partial-stack fallback, ADRC fallback, or single-frame fallback.
- 3.5 s is only a terminal watchdog for a post-shutter role; readiness completes immediately.
- One active Motion reconstruction remains the maximum. A busy shutter press becomes one deferred intent instead of being discarded; repeated busy taps collapse to one future capture.
- Deferred intent replays only on the same live Motion camera generation/physical camera and after RAW readiness; camera/mode/session changes cancel safely.
- Super Res never owns capture accounting. It consumes the same exact immutable NORMAL set downstream; SHORT/LONG remain excluded from direct CFA/subpixel/detail evidence and DNG remains NORMAL-only.
- Every accepted Motion shutter must terminate in an immutable batch or an explicit user-visible capture failure; silent application drops are forbidden.
