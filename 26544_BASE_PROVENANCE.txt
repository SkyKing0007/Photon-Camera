Original Photon comparison used as engineering control
------------------------------------------------------
Upstream PhotonCamera RAW16Saver/SaverImplementation/ImageFrame uses the safe ownership pattern:
acquire RAW Image -> deep app-owned copy -> Image.close() before callback ownership is released.
26543 diverged by handing live Camera2 Images to asynchronous disk workers. The tested 26543
fresh non-ZSL RAW ImageReader has maxImages=3 while the 26543 spool permitted three live acquired
Images. 26544 restores immediate Camera2 Image release discipline without restoring Photon Night
exposure, frame policy, merge, demosaic, denoise, tone, or color processing.
