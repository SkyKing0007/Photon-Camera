Why the scope is 10 rather than the older interrupted 12-file prototype
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Exact 26526 call-graph auditing proved that saveNormalized16StackedRaw() is used by the still
stacked-DNG path and is not the RawVideo path. Embedded preview can therefore be opt-in at the
stacked still saver without modifying HdrxProcessor or RawVideoProcessor merely to carry a boolean.
Those two owners remain byte-identical to successful 26526.

Spatial/MGC correction
----------------------
The active owner remains GlesIris26521SpatialRgbStacker/Shaders. Dormant GlesMgcRaw Spatial
owners remain frozen. The correction removes the extra Bayer-alignment conversion, uses the final
AlignAlt alignment field consistently, corrects RAW/2 -> RAW/4 dimensions, moves DilateMask before
filtering, downsamples pixel difference 2x into the same merge-weight domain, uses acceptance-weight
semantics including acceptance-preserving max(), and adds initial/final acceptance telemetry.
No tone, color, sharpening, denoise, exposure, or burst-scheduling tuning is introduced here.

Zoom / gesture / routing correction
-----------------------------------
Requested zoom and displayed zoom are separated across optical handoffs. Handoffs advance only to
the adjacent optical anchor, including fast gestures that cross multiple anchors. Reverse hysteresis
is retained. Pinch establishment cancels the old single-finger gesture so a completed pinch cannot
fall through into a focus tap. Camera routing prefers direct physical access, then logical camera
plus physical output binding, then logical-only zoom. Each configured session carries immutable
route identity so logical CameraDevice callbacks can prove the actual physical owner. Preview is
covered during restart, normal reveal waits for route proof, and a 1.6 second fail-safe prevents a
permanently covered viewfinder. Topology diagnostics are retained for later camera-ID5 investigation.

DNG preview correction
----------------------
The full stacked 16-bit Bayer RAW and tested 26525 DefaultCrop path are preserved. Still stacked DNG
optionally adds a reduced RGB preview and converts the secondary TinyDNG IFD into a real TIFF/DNG
SubIFD relationship using tag 330. The ordinary next-IFD chain is zeroed after promotion. The main
Bayer strip offset remains the returned main RAW strip. RawVideo remains preview-disabled by default.
