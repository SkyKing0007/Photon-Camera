26692 permanent device/runtime regressions

1. 26691 Xiaomi Spektra still failure:
   Camera2 reached a real 4096x3072 RAW_SENSOR still with a valid 17x13 still LSC, then native RCD failed at Vulkan external-memory allocation with VK_ERROR_INVALID_EXTERNAL_HANDLE (-1000072003).
   Regression: RAW_SENSOR still/preview must have a direct row-stride native-upload carrier and may not depend exclusively on Android HardwareBuffer external import.

2. Standalone 1.1.2 RAW routing parity:
   Prefer RAW10 when the route advertises it, then RAW12, with RAW_SENSOR as a functional fallback. Format preference must precede capture raster-area preference.

3. RAW_SENSOR fallback:
   RAW_SENSOR remains RAW_SENSOR; copy row-by-row into a direct rowStride*height carrier only when Android omits final-row padding. Never convert it to RAW10/12. A RAW_SENSOR-only route must remain able to reach native RCD/JPEG.

4. 26691 LSC correction remains permanent:
   Preview LSC is optional at shutter. Still-frame LSC outranks same-route preview LSC; NONE is allowed and must not reject capture.

5. Spektra histogram:
   Native histogramPixels output is a rendered sample source, not a finished histogram bitmap. It must be reduced to RGB bins and drawn with Photo histogram presentation. No Spektra toggle; ordinary Iris histogram bytes remain unchanged.

6. Video/RAW Video UI:
   Recording visuals/behavior may differ, but shutter container, inner shutter scale, gallery, camera switch, lens row, manual toggle/palette/sliders must use the Photo geometry owner. Old -48dp/-30dp/88dp/74dp video geometry may not return.

7. Unspektra exposure:
   Do not replace the recovered Unspektra sensor-AE owner with Android AE and do not add speculative +EV compensation. The existing 26691 Unspektra AE implementation is protected in 26692.
