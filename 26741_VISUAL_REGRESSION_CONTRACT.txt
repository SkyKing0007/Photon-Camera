26741 device visual contract
1. Preserve the substantial 26740 improvement in 2x font/edge geometry; no return of horizontal zipper/stair-step structure.
2. Neutral barcode/black-white periodic structure must not develop cyan/yellow false chroma at 1x, Motion zoom, or Super Res ON.
3. Clipped/flattened white chandelier, lamp, chrome, reflection, and sun-glint regions must fail neutral instead of teal/cyan/pink/yellow when independent unclipped same-surface color evidence is absent.
4. Real bright colored material may retain color only when independently supported below highlight headroom; reconstructed highlight hue cannot prove itself.
5. Preserve genuine small colored text/logos, foliage, fabric/denim, hair, stitching and other coherent real color/detail from 26729+.
6. No global chroma muting/denoise increase, no chroma sharpening, no semantic barcode/text special case, no Plan B linear SR.
7. Super Res ON GPU and native CPU fallback must obey the same confidence-controlled detail publication principle.
8. Motion/digital-zoom 26740 confidence architecture must remain unchanged.
9. UHDR/DNG/vendor/protected domains remain unchanged unless explicitly declared; SDR base behavior remains inherited.
