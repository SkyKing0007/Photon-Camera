26580 V1 MICRO-COLOR / SR NEUTRAL-TEXT REFINEMENT REGRESSION CONTRACT

DEVICE EVIDENCE FROM SUCCESSFUL 26579
- Tiny multicolor world-flag circles improve versus 26578 but remain somewhat muted in both normal and Super Res captures.
- At equivalent physical scale, Super Res preserves additional luma/detail but still shows a wider residual purple/magenta/cyan chroma footprint around neutral dark glyphs than the normal image.
- Main-camera SR completes about 24.0s with GPU reconstruction + GPU ASYNC_FENCE publication.
- Tele SR completes about 36.5s with the same GPU publication architecture.
- Therefore GPU publication, SR luma/alignment, Sabre/HDR/DNG/Night ownership are frozen in this refinement.

REQUIRED SHARED VGN BEHAVIOR
1. Inherit the complete 26578/26579 fail-closed color contract.
2. A tiny real multicolor print may prove itself by compact 2-D chroma occupancy even when neighboring hues disagree.
3. Near-field chroma must occupy both image axes and have nonzero minor second moment to earn compact-area protection.
4. Horizontal or diagonal one-pixel chromatic ribbons must not earn the compact-area veto.
5. Weak/moderate Bayer-phase resemblance inside a compact multicolor print must not erase that print.
6. An overwhelming repeated near-opposite/far-matching Bayer-phase signature must still extinguish the new veto so the inherited neutral purple/green fringe remains correctable.
7. Tiny isolated saturated color with no false-color proof remains untouched.
8. Colored contours/text strokes and foliage-versus-sky remain protected.
9. No global saturation increase, new hue, luma modification, geometry change, blur or sharpening is allowed.

REQUIRED TRUE2X CHROMA BEHAVIOR
1. true2x flow/refinement/alignment and direct-CFA luma/detail math are byte-identical to successful 26579.
2. Native Sabre/VGN remains the only chroma authority; direct-CFA remains luma-only.
3. Same-material weighting must strongly suppress samples on the opposite luma/material side; the old meaningful cross-edge weight floor is forbidden.
4. High-resolution direct-CFA luma may select material side only in proportion to existing SR confidence; it must never contribute chroma.
5. On a colored object/stroke side, supported native VGN chroma must be retained without exceeding the strongest local native chroma magnitude.
6. On a neutral side of a strong edge, if at least three local native samples are neutral and the luma-selected owner is neutral, opposite-side chroma leakage must be pulled toward that existing neutral owner chroma.
7. A spatially owned tiny colored sample must veto the neutral-side clamp so a flag/logo/colored glyph is not erased.
8. Flat colored surfaces remain exact bilinear/pass-through in chroma and exact guide luminance.
9. Neutral black/white glyphs must not acquire chroma; colored glyphs must keep their owned color inside the stroke.
10. Output luminance remains exactly the existing bilinear-guide/direct-CFA factor result; this build does not sharpen, move or repaint geometry.

PAIRED PERMANENT SYNTHETIC REGRESSIONS
- inherited neutral Bayer-phase purple/green cross remains >90% eligible for cleanup;
- compact 3x3 multicolor flag/logo area is preserved;
- compact 2x2 multicolor micro-object is preserved;
- horizontal and diagonal chromatic ribbons do not earn 2-D object protection;
- foliage/sky boundary remains protected;
- SR flat color is exact pass-through;
- SR colored material side retains supported chroma without saturation invention;
- SR neutral side excludes opposite colored-object chroma;
- SR neutral glyph outside-edge purple/cyan footprint is materially reduced;
- SR colored glyph/object owner is not neutral-clamped.

NO-CHANGE DOMAINS
- no native C++ / GPU publication changes;
- no Sabre merge/frame-admission changes;
- no true2x alignment/flow/detail-confidence changes;
- no exposure/HDR/frame-policy changes;
- no DNG/Night/UHDR changes;
- no RCD/demosaic resurrection;
- no sharpening or global saturation boost.
