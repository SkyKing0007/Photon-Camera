IRIS 26564 PERMANENT REGRESSION — TRUE2X UHDR OWNERSHIP

Failure found during candidate audit:
Motion true-2x initially reused the proven native-size 1x gain map with a 2x primary.
That breaks the established 1:1 primary/gain-map sampling contract and would require
spatial resampling of HDR gain information.

Permanent conditions for every applicable future handoff:
1. Motion true-2x with UHDR must generate one gain sample for every final true-2x primary pixel.
2. Motion gain is derived from the same true-2x pre-render extended-linear HDR and rendered SDR
   values used for that primary pixel; no native-size bitmap/gainmap resampling may define it.
3. Motion true-2x gain-map dimensions must equal final true-2x primary dimensions.
4. Night must NOT use the Motion true-2x gain generator. Night retains the proven
   IrisNightUltraHdr post-Jin rebased gain-map ownership.
5. The encoder must package the original Gainmap metadata while replacing only Motion's
   spatial gain payload with the 1:1 true-2x payload.
